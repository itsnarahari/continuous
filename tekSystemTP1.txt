TEKsystems TP1 – Actual Interview Questions
1. Explain your current project, day-to-day responsibilities, and the Java, Groovy, and Spring features you use.
2. Are you using a CI/CD pipeline?
3. How do you manage application.yml configurations across DEV, UAT, and production?
4. Where are you deploying your application? Cloud or on-premises?
5. What Java 17 features have you used in your current project?
6. What are virtual threads, and what benefits do they provide? What problem do they solve?
7. Do you know the limitations of virtual threads?
8. Can virtual threads improve CPU-intensive operations?
9. Can you explain the record classes you have used?
10. Do you have microservices architecture in your current project? How do the services communicate?
11. How do you avoid duplicate message processing in Kafka?
12. What happens when Kafka consumer lag increases?
13. If a consumer processes 1,000 messages per second but suddenly drops to 100, what would you debug?
14. How would you identify whether Kafka or the database is the bottleneck?
15. What happens if a Kafka producer sends messages while the broker is unavailable?
16. Would you configure monitoring or alerting for this?
17. If one Kafka partition receives 90% of the traffic and the other partitions are almost empty, how would you solve it?
18. Are you using a load balancer or API Gateway in your current project?
19. How does the load balancer distribute traffic?
20. How do you identify increased traffic? Do you have monitoring or autoscaling configured?

27. Do you know the internal working of @Transactional?

29. Where would you use the Saga pattern?
30. What does the Saga pattern achieve?
Coding
