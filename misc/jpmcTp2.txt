TP2 interview — interviewer questions
1. Introduction and project
1. Where are you from?
2. How are you?
3. Can you tell me about yourself?
4. What kind of company is Celsior Technologies — service-based or product-based?
5. What is Farm Bureau?
6. What kind of domain is Farm Bureau?
7. Can you explain your PIC system?
8. Can you explain the project?
9. How many producers are there?
10. Is this system also in production?
2. Spring Batch
11. In the middle of Spring Batch processing, if it fails, how can you restart it and make it resilient?
12. What is a Step in Spring Batch?
13. After processing a record, does Spring Batch commit that record as completed?
14. Do you process records using chunks?
15. Suppose you have 1,000 records and the batch fails around the 350th record. Will Spring Batch roll back those 50 records in the current chunk?
16. If a chunk fails, how are the failed records recorded?
17. Who takes care of recording the failure?
18. How do you identify the particular step or chunk that failed?
19. How will you restart reading from the failed point?
20. Are you emitting events while processing these records?
3. Kafka, Outbox and DLQ
21. Who triggers the report?
22. How many events are emitted as part of your day-to-day reporting?
23. If the report publishing process fails, how do you retrieve and process messages from the failed point?
24. Suppose five million records are pending in the Outbox. How would you handle that?
25. If the Outbox batch fails at the same point every time, how would you handle it?
26. If the batch retries after 15 minutes but keeps failing at the same point, what would you do?
27. Is the problem related to code, a decision, or the data?
28. Is that failure happening during consumption?
29. I am asking about the producer side. How do you handle producer-side failures?
30. If the Outbox keeps filling up and you are approaching the SLA, how would you handle it?
4. Kafka consumers, partitions and scaling
31. How do you handle consumer-side problems?
32. How would you process messages in parallel?
33. How does Kafka consumer rebalancing work when a consumer fails?
34. If you have 10 partitions and 9 consumers, how are the partitions distributed?
35. Suppose you scale consumers to 100 or 1,000 instances, but still cannot process all Kafka messages within the SLA. What could be blocking you?
36. What is consumer lag?
37. If CPU and memory are fine but consumer lag is still high, what could be the reason?
38. Where are your applications hosted — cloud or on-premises?
39. Is Kafka also hosted in the cloud?
40. How do you monitor consumer lag?
41. How do you scale consumers automatically based on consumer lag?
42. How would you trigger automatic scaling based on Kafka metrics?
5. Kafka producer performance and configuration
43. How do you improve Kafka producer performance?
44. Suppose you already have 100 instances, but Kafka publishing is still slow. What would you investigate?
45. How does Kafka rebalancing impact the producer and consumer?
46. How does increasing the number of partitions affect the producer?
47. How does increasing the number of partitions affect the consumer?
48. How do you configure producer acknowledgements?
49. What are acks=0, acks=1 and acks=all?
50. How do batch.size and linger.ms improve producer performance?
51. How do you handle idempotency in Kafka?
6. Kafka Inbox and duplicate processing
52. Suppose multiple consumer instances receive the same event and try to insert its event ID into the Inbox table simultaneously. What happens?
53. How would you prevent multiple instances from processing the same event?
54. If one consumer instance crashes before committing the offset, another instance receives the same event. How can you ensure that the event is not processed twice?
55. How can you guarantee that the same event is not processed again?
7. Agile, team and tools
56. Do you work in Scrum sprints or Kanban?
57. What is your role in the team?
58. What is the team size?
59. Do you use AI tools such as GitHub Copilot or Microsoft Copilot?
8. Spring Security and JWT
60. How do you handle authentication and authorization in your REST APIs?
61. Who provides the JWT token?
62. What happens when a user has a valid JWT but does not have permission to access a particular resource?
9. REST API idempotency and rate limiting
63. How do you handle idempotency in REST APIs?
64. Do you need idempotency for GET, POST and PUT?
65. If a client continuously invokes your REST API and creates a heavy load, how do you control the traffic?
66. What is Feign Client?
67. When calling an external service using Feign Client, what do you need to ensure a successful request and response?
68. How do you get the token when one service calls another service?
69. How do you pass the token to the external service?
70. What is the difference between query parameters and path parameters?
71. What are request headers?
10. Production support and troubleshooting
72. Have you worked on production support?
73. How do you debug a production issue?
74. How do you perform root-cause analysis?
75. How do you communicate the issue and findings to the team or leadership?

76. Have you ever disagreed with another developer's technical approach? How did you handle it?
77. What would you do if another developer does not agree with your approach?

79. Have you received any critical or negative feedback from your manager or peers?
80. How did you respond to that feedback?
81. Suppose requirements change in the middle of a sprint and change again near the end of the sprint. How would you handle the impact on delivery?
82. What would you do if the Product Owner frequently changes requirements and provides limited information?
83. Why did you leave your previous company?
84. Do you currently have any other offers?
85. What is your current CTC?
86. What do you think about AI? Will it replace software developers?
87. If you join a team and do not get enough support from team members, how would you overcome the situation?
88. What are your career aspirations?
89. How do you plan to achieve your career goals?
90. Are you learning new technologies outside your current project?
91. Have you worked with AWS EC2?
92. Have you worked with AWS in your previous projects?
